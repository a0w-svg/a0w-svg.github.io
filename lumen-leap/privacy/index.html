Lumen Leap - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-11
Developer: a0wsvg
Contact: a0wsvg123@proton.me

In short: Lumen Leap has no accounts and no analytics of its own. Your game progress stays on your device. The developer does not collect or receive your personal data. The game shows ads through third-party advertising software, and that software may collect certain data as described below.

1. Who we are

Lumen Leap ("the game", "the app") is a free arcade game for Android published by a0wsvg ("we", "us"). You can contact us at a0wsvg123@proton.me.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information on our own servers. The game has no user accounts, no sign-in, no chat or social features, and no analytics or crash-reporting software added by us.

The following is stored only on your device (in the app's local storage) and is never sent to us: your gems, high score, game statistics, unlocked and selected skins, daily gift and daily mission progress, sound, music and haptics settings, and your ad-consent choice. Deleting the app's data or uninstalling the game removes it.

3. Advertising and third-party SDKs

Lumen Leap is free and supported by ads. It uses Unity LevelPlay (an ad mediation service from Unity, which includes ironSource technology) to show rewarded videos, interstitial ads and banner ads. LevelPlay includes Unity Ads and may load additional ad networks that we enable in the LevelPlay dashboard.

These third parties act on their own behalf as ad providers. When an ad is requested or shown, they may automatically collect and use data from your device, which can include:

  • your device's advertising ID (for example the Google Advertising ID on Android);
  • device and software information (model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type, mobile carrier, and similar technical identifiers);
  • your IP address and the approximate location (country or region, or city level) derived from it; the game does not ask for precise GPS location;
  • information about how you interact with ads (impressions, clicks, video views, rewards earned) and about the app session in which the ad was shown;
  • diagnostic information, such as ad load failures and SDK performance data.

They use this data to select and show ads (including personalised ads if you allow them), to limit how often ads are shown, to measure ad performance, to prevent fraud and abuse, and to pay for ad delivery. The data is sent over the internet to the ad companies and their partners and is handled under their own privacy policies.

Ad networks. The networks that can serve ads depend on how the developer configures LevelPlay. At the time of this policy these are: Unity Ads. If we enable further networks, we will list them here. The privacy policy of each network applies to data it collects.

Useful links:

  • Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource information is covered by the Unity Privacy Policy above.
  • Google (Android advertising ID and ad personalisation settings): https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Your choices

  • Consent in the game. The first time you open the game, you are asked whether ads may be personalised (Allow personalised or Non-personalised only). The ad software is not started until you have made this choice. Your choice is passed to the ad provider as consent for personalised ads and, where applicable, as a "do not sell or share" signal.
  • Changing your choice. Open Settings in the game and tap AD PRIVACY to see the choice again and change it at any time.
  • Device settings. On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact path varies by device). If you delete the advertising ID, ads can still be shown but will not be based on it.
  • Non-personalised ads are still ads. They may use contextual information, but you will see the same number of ads.

5. Legal bases and your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, ad providers process your advertising data on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw as described above. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict certain processing, to opt out of sale or sharing for targeted advertising, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Because we do not hold your personal data, we cannot look up, correct or delete it. To exercise your rights regarding data held by an ad provider, please contact that provider using the links above, or write to us at a0wsvg123@proton.me and we will point you to the right place. To delete the data stored on your device, clear the app's storage or uninstall the game.

6. How to request deletion of your data (Lumen Leap, developer: a0wsvg)

Data on your device. All game data (gems, high score, statistics, skins, daily gift and mission progress, settings and your ad-consent choice) is stored only on your phone. To delete it immediately, open Android Settings > Apps > Lumen Leap > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the game. We keep no copy, so nothing is retained by us.

Data collected by the ad providers. To ask for deletion of advertising data linked to your device:

  1. Send an email to a0wsvg123@proton.me with the subject "Lumen Leap data deletion request".
  2. Include your device's advertising ID (Android Settings > Privacy > Ads or Settings > Google > Ads), because the ad providers identify data only by this ID.
  3. Within 30 days we forward your request to Unity (LevelPlay and Unity Ads) and confirm by email. You can also contact Unity directly through its privacy request form linked from https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

What is deleted and what is kept. Ad providers delete the advertising data tied to that advertising ID (device identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics). They may keep limited records where the law requires it, or for fraud prevention and billing, for the periods stated in their own privacy policies. We keep your email only as long as needed to answer the request and delete it afterwards. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings at any time, which unlinks future ads from past data.

7. Children

Lumen Leap is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age of digital consent in your country, if higher), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us at a0wsvg123@proton.me.

8. Data security and retention

Game data stays on your device and is protected by the device's own security. We keep no copy. Retention of data by ad providers is set by their own policies.

9. Data transfers

Ad providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States and Israel. Their privacy policies describe the safeguards they use for international transfers.

10. Links and sharing

The game can open the Play Store (to rate the game), this policy in your browser, and the Android share sheet when you choose to share your score. What happens after that is governed by the apps you choose.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this address. If the change is material (for example, a new data practice), we will also describe it in the app's update notes.
